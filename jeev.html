Project JEEV

In This Life, Save A Life

Animal Welfare Initiative

Compassion for Every Living Being

Project Jeev is dedicated to protecting and caring for stray animals through feeding programs, rescue initiatives, shelter support, and community awareness. We believe every living being deserves care, safety, and dignity.

With a dedicated volunteer network, Project Jeev feeds 50+ animals daily, provides shelter referrals, and organises rescue drives that give injured and abandoned animals a second chance at life.

  • Feeding Stray Animals — daily organised feeding drives
  • Shelter Support — foster network and temporary shelter referrals
  • Rescue Activities — emergency rescue for injured animals
  • Animal Care Awareness — community education on animal welfare

Key Activities

Our Animal Welfare Work

Four core activities ensuring stray and injured animals receive the care, food, and shelter they deserve.

Feeding Strays

Daily feeding drives providing nutritious food and clean water to stray dogs, cats, and other animals.

  • Daily feeding routes
  • Nutritious pet food
  • Water bowl placement
  • Seasonal feeding camps

Provide Shelter

Organising temporary shelter, foster home networks, and safe spaces for rescued and abandoned animals.

  • Foster home network
  • Temporary shelters
  • Adoption drives
  • Safe release zones

Rescue Activities

Emergency rescue operations for injured and abandoned animals, with vet coordination for medical care.

  • Emergency rescue calls
  • Wound treatment
  • Vet coordination
  • Sterilisation support

Animal Care Awareness

Community education campaigns raising awareness about animal rights, kindness, and responsible pet ownership.

  • Anti-cruelty campaigns
  • School awareness drives
  • Social media outreach
  • Volunteer mobilisation
