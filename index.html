Who We Are

About InAmigos Foundation

A Section 8 registered non-profit working across India for social welfare and sustainable development.

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organisation founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, headquartered in Chhattisgarh, India.

The organisation works across India to create meaningful social impact through education, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, women empowerment, community welfare, and youth skill development. Through dedicated volunteers, interns, and social initiatives, InAmigos Foundation strives to build a more inclusive, compassionate, and empowered society.
