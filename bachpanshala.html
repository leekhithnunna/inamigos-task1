Project BACHPANSHALA

Shaping Dreams, Building Futures

Education & Child Development

Quality Education for Every Child

BachpanShala focuses on educating children from underprivileged communities by providing educational support, digital literacy, mentorship, and life skills. We believe every child — regardless of socioeconomic background — deserves access to quality learning and a nurturing environment.

Through volunteer-led learning sessions, digital literacy programmes, and community engagement, BachpanShala creates safe, inclusive spaces where children can learn, explore, and dream without barriers.

  • School Education Support — tutoring and academic assistance
  • Digital Literacy Training — basic computing and internet skills
  • Mentorship Programs — one-on-one guidance from trained volunteers
  • Child Development Activities — arts, sports, and creativity
  • Community Learning Camps — regular learning drive events

Key Activities

Our Education Programmes

Four core areas delivering holistic child development through dedicated volunteers and community engagement.

School Education Support

Volunteer-driven tutoring sessions, free study materials, and exam prep coaching for school-going children.

  • Daily teaching sessions
  • Homework support
  • Free study kits
  • Exam coaching

Digital Literacy Training

Basic computer skills, internet literacy, and digital safety education for children from non-digital households.

  • Computer basics
  • Internet safety
  • Typing & MS Office
  • Educational apps

Mentorship Programs

Pairing children with trained mentors who guide academics, career awareness, and life skills development.

  • One-on-one mentorship
  • Career awareness
  • Life skills coaching
  • Confidence building

Community Learning Camps

Organised weekend learning camps and community drives creating a culture of education in underserved areas.

  • Weekend learning camps
  • Parent awareness meets
  • School enrolment drives
  • Cultural events

What Children Gain

BachpanShala delivers measurable outcomes across education, health, development, and life skills.

Life Skills
