Project Prakriti

Creating A Greener Tomorrow

For Our Planet

Nurturing Nature, Sustaining Life

Project Prakriti promotes environmental sustainability through plantation drives, environmental awareness campaigns, and eco-friendly practices. We believe protecting nature is not a choice — it's our collective responsibility.

From planting saplings to organising beach and river cleanups, from educating school children about climate change to inspiring communities to adopt sustainable living, Prakriti is on a mission to leave behind a greener, cleaner planet for future generations.

  • Mass tree plantation drives across cities
  • River, beach, and public space cleanliness drives
  • Environmental awareness in schools and colleges
  • Plastic-free and eco-friendly lifestyle campaigns
  • World Environment Day & Earth Day events

Our Initiatives

Green Action Areas

Four interconnected pillars working together to restore and protect our natural environment.

Tree Plantation

Large-scale plantation drives in urban areas, schools, parks, and rural lands to increase green cover.

  • City plantation drives
  • School green campaigns
  • Sapling distribution
  • Survival monitoring

Environmental Awareness

Education campaigns, workshops, and events that inspire individuals to adopt eco-friendly behaviours.

  • School & college workshops
  • Social media campaigns
  • Documentary screenings
  • Green pledge drives

Cleanliness Drives

Organised Swachh Bharat-inspired clean-up events at beaches, rivers, parks, and residential areas.

  • Beach cleanups
  • River bank drives
  • Park & public spaces
  • Waste segregation awareness

Sustainable Living

Promoting zero-waste lifestyles, plastic-free habits, and community composting and upcycling projects.

  • Plastic-free challenges
  • Composting workshops
  • Upcycling projects
  • Eco-friendly product drives
