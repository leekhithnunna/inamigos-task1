Project Vikas

Transforming Careers, One Internship At A Time

Youth Empowerment

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Project Vikas empowers youth through internships, skill development, webinars, workshops, career guidance, and professional development opportunities. We bridge the gap between education and employment for India's ambitious young generation.

With over 1,000 internship opportunities created and 30,000+ interns onboarded, Vikas is one of InAmigos Foundation's most impactful programmes — giving young professionals the real-world experience, mentorship, and network they need to launch their careers.

  • Internship placements across diverse domains
  • Resume building and LinkedIn profile workshops
  • Mock interview and interview preparation sessions
  • Industry webinars and skill development workshops
  • Certificates, letters of recommendation, and networking

Career Programmes

What Vikas Offers

Five career-building pathways designed to transform fresh graduates into industry-ready professionals.

Internships

Real-world internship opportunities across HR, marketing, design, content, social media, tech, and NGO management.

  • Remote & on-site roles
  • Stipend-based positions
  • Certificate of completion
  • Letter of recommendation

Career Development

Structured guidance to help young professionals identify their strengths, set goals, and build a career roadmap.

  • Career counselling
  • Goal-setting workshops
  • Industry exposure
  • Networking sessions

Workshops & Webinars

Expert-led online and offline workshops on in-demand skills, industry trends, and professional development topics.

  • Digital marketing
  • Content creation
  • Leadership skills
  • Entrepreneurship basics

Resume & Interview Prep

One-on-one and group sessions to perfect resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, and interview technique.

  • ATS-optimised resumes
  • Cover letter writing
  • Mock interviews
  • LinkedIn optimisation
