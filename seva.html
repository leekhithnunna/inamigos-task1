Project SEVA

Serving Humanity Through Compassion

Community Support

Reducing Hunger & Clothing Deprivation

Project SEVA is the flagship initiative of InAmigos Foundation dedicated to reducing hunger and clothing deprivation among underprivileged communities. We believe every person deserves nutritious food, dignified clothing, and essential support.

Through organised food distribution drives, dry ration kits, and clothing collection campaigns, Project Seva directly reaches thousands of beneficiaries across multiple cities, providing immediate, dignified support to the most vulnerable.

  • Food Distribution Drives — fresh cooked meals at community events
  • Dry Ration Support — monthly ration kits for families in need
  • Community Feeding Programs — organised feeding drives
  • Clothing Distribution — quality garments and school uniforms
  • Blanket Drives — winter support for homeless individuals
  • Support for Homeless Families — holistic welfare assistance

Key Activities

What We Do

Four interconnected pillars of action directly addressing the most fundamental human needs.

Food Distribution Drives

Organised food drives at community locations distributing fresh cooked meals and nutritious food packets.

  • Fresh cooked meals at camps
  • Dry ration kits for families
  • Community feeding events
  • Festival special drives

Dry Ration Support

Monthly dry ration kit distribution ensuring families have access to essential food supplies consistently.

  • Monthly ration kits
  • Essential grocery items
  • Nutrition-balanced packages
  • Door-to-door support

Clothing Distribution

Collecting and distributing quality clothing, blankets, and footwear to underprivileged communities.

  • Clothing collection drives
  • Blanket distribution (winter)
  • Footwear support
  • School uniform assistance

Support for Homeless Families

Holistic welfare assistance for homeless families including food, shelter guidance, and essential resources.

  • Shelter support & guidance
  • Emergency relief kits
  • Hospital patient support
  • Old age home visits
