Women Empowerment

Empowering Women to Lead, Grow & Succeed

Project Udaan empowers women through skill development, entrepreneurship support, leadership training, financial awareness, and community engagement. We believe that when women are empowered, entire communities rise with them.

Through targeted workshops, mentorship programmes, and a strong community support network, Project Udaan has directly empowered over 900 women — equipping them with knowledge, skills, and confidence to achieve financial independence and become leaders of change in their communities.

  • Skill Development Training — tailoring, digital literacy, communication
  • Entrepreneurship Support — business basics and micro-enterprise guidance
  • Leadership Workshops — public speaking and community leadership
  • Women's Health Awareness — wellness, hygiene and mental health
  • Financial Independence Programs — savings, banking and legal rights

Key Activities

Five Pillars of Women Empowerment

Comprehensive programmes addressing skills, enterprise, leadership, health, and financial independence for women.

Skill Development Training

Practical workshops in tailoring, spoken English, digital literacy, computer basics, and professional communication skills.

  • Tailoring & handicraft
  • Digital literacy skills
  • Communication training
  • Vocational courses

Entrepreneurship Support

Business development training helping women start, manage, and grow their own micro-enterprises and home-based businesses.

  • Business planning basics
  • Marketing & sales skills
  • Govt. schemes access
  • Self-help group formation

Leadership Workshops

Building confident, articulate women leaders through public speaking, decision-making, and community leadership training.

  • Public speaking sessions
  • Team leadership training
  • Decision-making skills
  • Community leadership

Women's Health Awareness

Workshops on physical health, mental wellness, hygiene, reproductive health, and access to healthcare facilities.

  • Health & hygiene workshops
  • Mental wellness sessions
  • Nutrition awareness
  • Healthcare access support

Financial Independence Programs

Teaching savings habits, banking basics, legal rights, and government welfare schemes to achieve financial autonomy.

  • Savings & banking basics
  • Legal rights awareness
  • Govt. welfare schemes
  • Financial planning basics

Community Engagement

Creating a strong peer support network where women mentor each other and collectively uplift their communities.

  • Peer support circles
  • Community awareness drives
  • Mentorship networks
  • Collaborative events
